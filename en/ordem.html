The Order

A Masonic Christian organisation dedicated to humility, charity and service.

Institutional Character

Founded in 1973, the Commemorative Order of St Thomas of Acon is a British Masonic Christian organisation dedicated to preserving a tradition that goes back to the Crusades and the martyrdom of Thomas Becket. The Order operates independently and is guided by the motto Humilitas et Caritas.

The Order is governed by a Central Authority based in England and is organised into regional Provinces. Each Province is responsible for its local Chapels, which function as centres of spiritual, educational and charitable activity.

Membership

Candidates must be Master Masons who are also Knights Templar, from jurisdictions recognised by the United Grand Lodge of England (UGLE) and the Great Priory of England.

Charity and Service

Charity is central to the identity of the Order. Our Chapels take part in social assistance, support for vulnerable communities and moral education grounded in Christian principles.

Our Principles

Humility

Recognising our limitations and serving without seeking personal recognition. The spiritual foundation of all action.

Charity

Active compassion and service to others. A commitment to social assistance, moral education and the relief of suffering.

Justice

Integrity of character and fairness in relationships. Defending the weak and acting according to Christian principles.

Faith

A Christian foundation. Trust in God and dedication to eternal values that transcend worldly interests.
