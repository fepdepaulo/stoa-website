Regalia

The vestments and insignia used in the Order’s ceremonies.

Mantle

Mantle

White hooded mantle, fastened with a chain and gold scallop-shell clasps, with an embroidered red cross and central shell.

Gauntlets

Gauntlets

White leather gauntlets with a high cuff and a red cross applied to the cuff.

Gauntlets

A pair of white gauntlets with a red cross on each cuff.

Cap

Cap

Cylindrical cap in claret velvet with a gold scallop shell on the side.

Tunic

Tunic

White high-collared tunic with a red cross and a gold scallop shell on the chest.

Cross of Past Worthy Master

Cross of Past Worthy Master

Copper cross with interlaced ornamentation and a central scallop shell.
