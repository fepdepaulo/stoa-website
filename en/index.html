The Commemorative Order of St Thomas of Acon

A Masonic Christian organisation from Britain, dedicated to humility, charity and service. Present in South America since 2014.

The Order

Founded in 1973 as a revival of a tradition dating back to the Crusades and the martyrdom of Thomas Becket, the Commemorative Order of St Thomas of Acon is an independent organisation dedicated to the preservation of principles of humility, charity and Christian service. We operate through regional Provinces; the Province of South America was constituted in January 2019 in Rio de Janeiro.

Humility

Recognising our limitations and serving without seeking personal recognition. The spiritual foundation of all action.

Charity

Active compassion and service to others. A commitment to social assistance, moral education and the relief of suffering.

Justice

Integrity of character and fairness in relationships. Defending the weak and acting according to Christian principles.

Faith

A Christian foundation. Trust in God and dedication to eternal values that transcend worldly interests.
