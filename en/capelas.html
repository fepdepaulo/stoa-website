Our Chapels

The Order works through local Chapels across South America. Each Chapel is a centre of religious, educational and charitable activity.

Provincial Banner

Stone-white field with the red cross of the Order, fimbriated of the field. In the first quarter, the scallop shell of the Order; in the second, the Southern Cross, with five gold stars.

Each Chapel bears this Provincial banner with its own device, placed at the fesse point of the cross.

Banner approved under §6.6 of the Constitutions of the Order.
