The Order

An English tradition of Christian chivalry, remembrance and service.

The Commemorative Order of St Thomas of Acon is an independent British Christian Masonic organisation, constituted in 1973 under a charter from the Grand Master's Council and organised in its contemporary form in 1974.

Its contemporary organisation followed some twenty years of research by John E. N. Walker at the Guildhall Library in London, where medieval records in French and Latin were preserved.

Humility and Charity

The motto HUMILITAS ET CARITAS guides a tradition of care, remembrance and assistance to others. The account of Acre is preserved by the Order as the founding memory of its ethic of service.

Membership by invitation

Admission is by invitation and subject to the Order's requirements. It is open to eligible members of a Knights Templar Preceptory or Commandery in recognised relations.
